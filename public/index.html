SECURITY AWARENESS

Small habits, stronger security

Use these checks whenever a message or website feels unexpected.

01

Pause before clicking

Unexpected urgency, prizes, threats or account warnings are reasons to slow down and verify through an official app or website.

02

Check the real domain

Read the hostname carefully. A familiar brand word inside a long domain does not mean the site belongs to that brand.

03

Protect every account

Use unique long passwords and enable multi-factor authentication, especially for email, banking and social accounts.

04

Never share verification codes

Do not share one-time codes, recovery codes or passwords with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

05

Keep devices updated

Install operating-system and browser updates, and download apps from trusted official stores.

06

Report, then verify

Report suspicious messages using the platform's official process. Contact the organization through a known, trusted channel.
